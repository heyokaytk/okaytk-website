Bio

OKAY TK is an award-winning DJ and event curator based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. He's known among friends and the DJ community for his personality, skill, and musical versatility, and for making every set feel like it was built for that crowd.

With 15+ years behind the decks, OKAY TK has been named Stylus DJ of the Year, made the Red Bull 3Style national finals three times, and won Coastal Culture Clash in 2024 and 2025. He's been heard on commercial radio (101.3 The Bounce, Z103.5) and community radio (88.1 CKDU), seen on CBC, CTV, and Eastlink, and shared the stage with artists including Rihanna, Ne-Yo, T-Pain, Jadakiss, and The Alchemist.

In Halifax he hosts Bump & Grind, a recurring R&B party with a monthly mix series. Whether it's a club night, a festival stage, or a corporate event, OKAY TK brings the right energy to the room.
